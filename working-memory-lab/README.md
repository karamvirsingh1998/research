# Working Memory Lab

An interactive page about short-term (working) memory: why it slips and what actually helps.

**Live:** https://karamvirsingh1998.github.io/research/working-memory-lab/

## What's inside
- **Memory self-check** – questionnaire, word recall, forward and backward digit span, and block tapping, with an age-compared report
- **Digit span test** – see how chunking raises your span
- **Forgetting curve** – the Brown–Peterson experiment, with distraction, sleep loss, and stress toggles
- **Neural drift** – a bump-attractor simulation of a prefrontal memory drifting over time
- **Evidence** – effect sizes for exercise, brain training, mindfulness, and supplements

## Scoring and limits
Scores use z-scores and percentiles against age-group norms. The Corsi norm under 60 is from Kessels et al. (2000); digit span and word-recall references are approximate, and the questionnaire is an informal checklist, not a validated scale. This is an educational tool, not a diagnostic test.
